# PlanCard

A subscription tier on the paywall: name, promise, price, annual equivalent and features.

**Props:** `tier` (`free`, `standard` or `premium`), `name`, `flag` (*Most popular*, *Best optimization*, *Coming soon*), `promise`, `price`, `period`, `subprice`, `features`, `selected`, `soon`.

- Standard is always the visual default: pre-selected, with *Most popular*.
- Premium is `soon` in Phase 1.
- Features list only what ships. For Standard: 5- and 7-day plans, ~30 imports a month and PDF, personalization, meal and ingredient swaps, daily rebalancing *proposals*, meal prep for 1–3 days, Economy Mode v1.
- The paywall around the cards must show price, billing period, trial terms, *Restore purchases*, and links to Terms and Privacy.
